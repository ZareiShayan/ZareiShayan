# Shayan Zarei

**Medical student | Computational neuroscience researcher | Neuroscience educator**

I am an MD student at Tehran University of Medical Sciences and Head of the Signal Department at Interdisciplinary Schools. My work focuses on neural population activity, electrophysiology, and quantitative approaches to brain and behavior. I also develop and teach practical courses in neural data analysis and signal processing.

## Featured research

| Project | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| [TVSD neural encoding](https://github.com/ZareiShayan/tvsd-encode) | Ongoing work on mean–covariance encoding models of image-driven spiking activity in the macaque ventral visual stream. |
| [Mean–covariance modeling](https://github.com/ZareiShayan/mean-cov-model) | Investigating neural mean activity and inter-neuronal dependency structure in dorsolateral prefrontal cortex during foraging in freely moving macaques. |

Additional ongoing work includes modeling relationships between EEG features and continuous behavioral measures in the Healthy Brain Network dataset. That repository is currently private.
