## Research, teaching, and leadership

### Research
- **Interdisciplinary Schools:** Lead research projects on neural encoding and EEG-based modeling; collaborate on neural population analyses in freely moving macaques.
- **EEG and brain stimulation:** Contributed to computational analysis of longitudinal EEG after theta-burst stimulation. [Read the preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.05.23.25328212v2); it is not presented here as a peer-reviewed publication.
- **Shefa Neuroscience Research Center:** Conducted in vivo and in vitro electrophysiology recordings of mouse hippocampal activity (2023–2024).

### Education and coordination
- **Interdisciplinary Schools:** Head of the Signal Department; design researcher and mentor training programs and coordinate neuroscience learning projects (2023–present).
- **Neural data instruction:** Teach [Neural Time Series Analysis](https://github.com/ZareiShayan/neural-time-series-analysis-2026) and have taught [Neural Data Analysis](https://github.com/ZareiShayan/neural-data-analysis-2025) and [Signal Processing with FieldTrip](https://github.com/ZareiShayan/signal-processing-fieldtrip-2025).
- **Neuromatch Academy:** Volunteer content creator for the Computational Neuroscience curriculum (2026).
